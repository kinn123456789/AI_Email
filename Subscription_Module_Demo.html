What this module does

When a family's subscription is cancelled, or their free trial runs out without converting, this module automatically notices — no one has to go looking for these families manually. It quietly writes a personalized "we'd love to have you back" draft in the background, ahead of time, so that when a staff member opens it up, the email is already there, already sounds right, and just needs a quick read and a click to send.

Features

What happens at /subscription-cancel.

Automatic Detection

Every minute, the latest cancelled subscriptions and expired free trials are pulled directly from Coral Academy's live records — nothing is entered by hand.

Drafts Ready Before You Open Them

A background job writes up to 5 win-back drafts every minute, so when staff open a row, the AI's suggestion is already sitting there — no waiting.

Personalized, But Safe

The real names of the parent and child are deliberately kept hidden while the AI is writing — the real names are only added back in afterward, once the draft is otherwise finished.

Review, Edit, Send

Open any row, read or tweak the draft, and send it straight away when it's ready.

Dismiss & Restore

Rows that don't need a follow-up can be dismissed out of the list, and brought right back if that turns out to be the wrong call.

Recent & All-Time Views

A fast rolling 3-month view for everyday use, plus a slower full-history view for looking further back.

Under the hood

Technical details

Data source subscription_cancel.py supabase_client.py

Reads directly from Coral Academy's own database tables (subscriptions, free trials, enrollments, class/batch info, learners, users, email-delivery status, and session activity) — there's no external API involved, and no manually maintained list to keep up to date.

Cadence

  • Every 1 minute: the list of cancelled subscriptions/expired trials is refreshed.
  • Every 1 minute (offset by 20 seconds): up to 5 AI drafts are pre-written for rows that don't have one yet, so the two jobs never run at the exact same moment and collide.
  • The cache is also warmed once immediately on startup, so the very first page load after a deploy isn't slow.

AI drafting & the name-hiding technique

The draft is written using Coral Academy's real subscription/trial details (plan type, sessions attended, cancelled vs. trial-expired) with a fixed, warm closing signature — it is not grounded in Help Center articles or past sent emails the way regular email replies are.

  • Before the AI writes anything, the real parent and learner names are swapped out for generic placeholder tokens — the AI only ever sees the placeholders, never the real names, while writing.
  • Once the draft comes back, the real names are substituted back in locally, so the final email reads naturally.
  • If the AI call fails for any reason, a safe, pre-written fallback email is used instead so a draft is never simply missing.
Known gap The swap-back of real names is currently a simple find-and-replace with no check afterward. If the AI ever rewords or drops a placeholder instead of repeating it exactly, nothing today would catch a leftover placeholder before someone hits send. Adding that check is on the list of things still to do.

Sending

Sending uses the same Gmail-based sending mechanism as the rest of the app, and every sent re-engagement email is logged so there's a record of exactly who was reached out to and when.
