Class: Around the Globe with Mr. Clark

Subject: lifeskills

Section: Description

Content:
In this interactive travel-themed virtual class, learners will embark on a virtual journey to explore a new destination each session. 

Using Nearpod, polls, and engaging activities, learners will navigate through cultural landmarks, historical sites, and natural wonders. They will explore essential travel questions—through geography lessons, language, currency, transportation, climate, and local customs—to gain a deeper understanding of global cultures. 

Through discussions and student-led exploration, learners will help shape the journey by suggesting places to visit, topics to research, and questions to investigate. This class is a fun mix of social studies, where we explore everything from geography and history to culture, religion, politics, natural wonders, wildlife, and even food!

Please find the week-on-week schedule for the next few weeks below, highlighting the destination for each week:

March 16-22: Israel - A complicated piece of geography
March 23-29: Palestine - A complicated piece of geography
March 30 - April 5: Jordan - Petra, the Dead Sea, ancient history, stunning desert landscapes, and rich culture.
April 6 - 12: Iraq - Babylon, and ancient Mesopotamia between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers
April 13-19: Syria - Damascus and the Cradle of Civilization
April 20-26: Turkey - The Ottoman Empire and a city in two continents.
April 27 - May 3: Greece - Philosophy? It's all Greek to me.

This class by Coral Academy is ideal for young learners wanting to study geography in an interactive way, blending online learning with real-world exploration. It’s a great way for kids to learn geography and explore different cultures as they virtually travel around the world.
