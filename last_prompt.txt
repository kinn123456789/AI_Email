What is Coral Unlimited Subscription?
Coral Unlimited is a limited-time subscription plan on Coral Academy that gives learners unlimited access to live virtual classes for just $40 per week per learner . Once subscribed, your learner can attend as many classes as they want without any additional per-class charges.
How to Use Coral Unlimited
Visit the Coral Academy website and sign in to your account.
Browse classes and select the ones you want to enroll in.
Enroll in multiple classes based on seat availability.
If a class is full, join the waitlist — active subscribers are automatically enrolled when seats open.
Payment & Renewal
Charges are processed to your chosen payment method.
The subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing cycle (every 7 days).
Weekly charges occur on the same day as your initial payment. For example, if you pay on a Monday, you'll be charged every Monday.
Activation Details
Your subscription activates immediately upon payment and renews automatically every 7 days.
Pausing Your Subscription
You can temporarily pause your subscription for travel, exams, vacations, or schedule breaks.
