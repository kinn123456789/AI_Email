Content:
Last Updated Date: July 31, 2025
At Coral Academy, we understand that sometimes schedules can change. Our policy aims to support instructors with flexibility while maintaining a consistent online learning experience for our learners.
Managing Class Cancellations
Rescheduling of a Class
Within your first 6 months, any cancelled classes must be rescheduled. Our platform team will assist with coordination.
Rescheduling builds your credibility as an instructor and minimizes disruption for learners.
Let Us Know in Advance
Please provide at least 48 hours' notice when possible.
For emergencies, contact us immediately.
Frequency of Class Cancellations
Keep cancellations minimal and ensure they have valid reasons.
Our platform will reach out if frequent cancellations are observed.
Procedure for Rescheduling
Email teachers@coralacademy.com with the reason for cancellation.
Our coordination team will identify a suitable rescheduled time.
Post an announcement with the reason to enrolled parents.
