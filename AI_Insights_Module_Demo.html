What this module does

Every time the AI classifies or drafts a reply — anywhere in the app, not just regular email — it's logged: which model, how many tokens, how long it took, and whether it succeeded. This module is the page where that record actually gets read.

Summary Stats

Total AI calls, how many failed, total tokens used, and average response time — a quick health check at a glance.

Broken Down by Category

Calls and error counts split by category (Classification, Billing, Admissions, Teacher, Reengagement, Trial Followup, etc.) — makes it easy to spot if one particular area is failing more than others.

Recent Errors

The last 20 AI failures, with full error text — the fastest way to notice something's actively going wrong.

Look Up One Draft's Log

Paste a specific email's Message-ID to see exactly what happened when its reply was generated — also reachable with one click from any email's detail page via "View AI Log."

Under the hood

Technical details

Where the logic actually lives database.py main.py

  • get_ai_insights() (database.py) — three queries: overall totals (calls/errors/tokens/avg response time), a per-category breakdown, and the last 20 errors. All three read straight from ai_logs.
  • get_ai_log_by_message_id() — powers the single-message lookup box.
  • get_ai_logs(), get_ai_log_categories(), delete_ai_log() — removed this session along with the full-log-table UI, since nothing else called them.
  • get_latest_reply_sources() — newly added this session; pulls which specific Knowledge Base articles and historical emails were actually used for a given reply, now shown directly on the email detail page next to the AI summary, not just here.
  • The route (@app.get("/ai-insights") in main.py) ties all of this together and also handles deleting a single log row.

What actually gets logged, and by what

  • Classification (ai_classifier.py) — logs on both success and failure, category always recorded as "Classification".
  • Reply generation (reply_generator.py) — logs with the email's real category (Billing, Admissions, etc.), plus which Knowledge Base articles and historical examples were used.
  • Teacher Portal messages — same classifier/generator, tagged teacher_portal:<message_id> so they're identifiable in the log.
  • Subscription re-engagement and Trial follow-up drafts — logged under their own categories ("Reengagement", "Trial Followup").
  • A logging failure itself can never crash the calling function — save_ai_log() catches its own errors and just prints, so a broken log write never destroys an already-generated draft.
