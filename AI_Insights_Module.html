Every time the AI classifies or drafts a reply — anywhere in the app, not just regular email — it's logged: which model, how many tokens and how long it took. This module is the page where that record actually gets read.

Under the hood

Technical details

Where the logic actually lives database.py main.py

  • get_ai_insights() (database.py) — two queries: overall totals (calls/tokens/avg response time), a per-category breakdown. Two queries read straight from ai_logs.
  • get_ai_logs(), get_ai_log_categories(), delete_ai_log() — removed this session along with the full-log-table UI, since nothing else called them.
  • get_latest_reply_sources() — pulls which specific Knowledge Base articles and historical emails were actually used for a given reply, now shown directly on the email detail page next to the AI summary, not just here.
  • The route (@app.get("/ai-insights") in main.py) ties all of this together and also handles deleting a single log row.

What actually gets logged in ai logs table for checking, and by what

  • Classification (ai_classifier.py) — logs on both success and failure, category always recorded as "Classification".
  • Reply generation (reply_generator.py) — logs with the email's real category (Billing, Admissions, etc.), plus which Knowledge Base articles and historical examples were used.
  • Teacher Portal messages — same classifier/generator so they're identifiable in the log.
  • Subscription re-engagement and Trial follow-up drafts — logged under their own categories ("Reengagement", "Trial Followup").
  • A logging failure itself can never crash the calling function — save_ai_log() catches its own errors and just prints, so a broken log write never destroys an already-generated draft.
